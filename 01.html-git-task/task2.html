Homemade Apple Pie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 6 to 8 apples, peeled, cored, and sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 package of pie crusts (or homemade crust)

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
  3. Roll out one pie crust and place it in a pie dish.
  4. Add the apple mixture to the crust and dot with butter.
  5. Cover with the second pie crust, seal the edges, and cut slits in the top for steam to escape.
  6. Bake for 15 minutes in the preheated oven. Reduce temperature to 350°F (175°C) and continue baking for 35 to 45 minutes.
  7. Let it cool before serving.

Serving Suggestion:

Serve your homemade apple pie warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream for a delicious treat!

Enjoy!
